# Transformers: The Last Knight (2017)

## A. Introduction

### Title
**Transformers: The Last Knight**

### Description
Transformers: The Last Knight is an action-packed movie about giant robots that can transform into vehicles and other machines. It follows the Autobots as they fight to protect Earth from dangerous enemies. The movie also reveals more about the history of Transformers and their connection to humanity.

### Genre
- Action
- Adventure
- Science Fiction

### Release Year
**2017**

---

## B. Synopsis

The story begins when humans and Transformers become enemies, causing the Autobots to be hunted across the world. Optimus Prime leaves Earth to find his creators and discover the truth about his origins. Meanwhile, Cade Yeager continues protecting the remaining Autobots while trying to survive in a world that no longer welcomes them.

As the story continues, Cade discovers an ancient secret connecting the Transformers to King Arthur and the Knights of the Round Table. He meets Vivian Wembley and Sir Edmund Burton, who help uncover the truth about an ancient Cybertronian artifact that could determine the fate of Earth. However, Optimus Prime is captured and manipulated by Quintessa, who turns him against his friends and makes him known as Nemesis Prime. Cade, Bumblebee, and their allies must find a way to bring Optimus back to his senses and prevent Earth from being destroyed.

## C. Characters

- **Optimus Prime** – The leader of the Autobots he is a brave and noble leader who fights to protect both humans and Transformers.
- **Bumblebee** – A loyal Autobot scout who helps protect Earth and plays an important role in bringing Optimus Prime back.
- **Cade Yeager** – An inventor who protects the Autobots and helps save Earth.
- **Vivian Wembley** – An Oxford professor who discovers her connection to Merlin and becomes important in finding the ancient staff.
- **Megatron** – The leader of the Decepticons who wants to obtain the ancient staff.

## D. Personal Review

### Why I Chose This Movie

I chose *Transformers: The Last Knight* because I have been a Transformers fan since I was a kid. Growing up, I enjoyed watching the movies and seeing the different robots transform into vehicles. My favorite character has always been **Optimus Prime** because of his leadership, bravery, and willingness to protect others.

### Three Things I Liked About the Movie

1. **Optimus Prime** – He has always been my favorite character. I like his design, fighting abilities, leadership, and the way he protects his friends.
2. **Visual Effects** – I enjoyed how detailed the Transformers looked, especially when they transformed into vehicles and fought against their enemies.
3. **Action Scenes** – The battles between the Autobots and Decepticons were exciting. The sound effects and music also made the scenes more enjoyable.

### My Rating

**9/10**

I would give this movie a 9 out of 10 because I really enjoyed its action scenes, characters, and visual effects. Although the story can sometimes be confusing, it is still one of the Transformers movies that I personally enjoy watching.

### Recommendation

I recommend this movie to people who enjoy science fiction, action, giant robots, and exciting battles. I especially recommend it to Transformers fans because it explores more of the history of the Autobots and their connection to Earth.

> Transformers has been one of my favorite movie franchises since childhood, and Optimus Prime will always be my favorite Autobot.
